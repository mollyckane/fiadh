Fiadh

pronounced “fee-ah”

An Irish name coming from the word ‘fia’ which means ‘deer’, representing the words “wild/free”.
A free, bilingual (English/Irish) web application designed as an all-in-one business toolkit for emerging and early-career freelance artists in Ireland.

Getting started

1
Register Create your free account in under a minute.
2
Personalise Choose your language, colour and font theme in Settings.
3
Start a task Try your first invoice, contract or income entry!

Our Mission

Fiadh supports emerging and early-career freelance artists in Ireland by making the business side of creative work feel manageable, not overwhelming.

  • Built for neurodivergent creatives, with plain language and low visual clutter.
  • Fully bilingual English/Irish, switchable instantly, no page reload.
  • Free to use, with no subscription or hidden paywalls.
