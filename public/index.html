A business toolkit for Irish artists

Fiadh

pronounced “fee-ah”

Wild ideas need calm support.

Adapted from the word fia, meaning “deer”. Fiadh is a free English-Irish toolkit for freelance artists in Ireland, created to make the business side of creative work feel simpler.

Why Fiadh exists

Our mission

Fiadh supports emerging and early-career freelance artists in Ireland by making the business side of creative work feel manageable, not overwhelming.

  • Built for neurodivergent creatives, using plain language and low visual clutter.
  • Fully bilingual English and Irish, switchable instantly without a page reload.
  • Free to use, with no subscription or hidden paywalls.
